Temporal Scope of Dataset
This repository contains a dataset covering market data from January 1, 2012, to April 30, 2024. While the original raw data spanned from 2010 to 2024, we've carefully filtered it to this specific period. The data prior to 2012 was excluded due to significant quality issues.

Our decision to focus on the 2012-2024 period was based on two main factors:

Data Quality: We noticed significant inconsistencies and noise in the data before 2012. Information regarding price and volume was either unreliable or had major discrepancies that would negatively affect our analysis. By excluding this older data, we ensure that the dataset is clean, reliable, and suitable for accurate analysis.

Methodological Requirements: Some of our analytical indicators, such as a 200-day moving average, require a consistent and complete time series. The filtered dataset provides a solid foundation for these calculations, preventing any gaps or errors that the earlier data would introduce.
